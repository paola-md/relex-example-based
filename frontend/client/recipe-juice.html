RELEX - Recipe Learning through Examples

A simple tool to help you improve your recipe writing skills.




Self-Reflection

First, please self-reflect on your recipe by answering the following questions.


1) On the right, you see an example recipe. What aspects of the example recipe do you find interesting, useful, convincing, and which not?


2) What deficiencies does your recipe have compared to the example recipe on the right?



Text Editor

Write your recipe here, click the "Analyze" button to recieve personalized tips shown in an example recipe.





Personal Dashboard

This dashboard shows a recipe chosen based on your text, exemplifying some improvements you can make.


Details

In the dashboard, you are seeing a recipe from another user. This recipe was chosen based on your recipe.

On the right, there are two reflection questions, please answer them.

Some parts of the text are highlighted. There are text boxes describing positive characteristics of the example recipe. These characteristics were taken from "The Recipe Writer's Handbook" (Gibbs & Baker, 2001).

  • Some descriptions might be relevant and accurate while others might not, you may close the ones that are not relevant.
  • After reading the example recipe and answering the questions, you may improve your recipe accordingly.

    • Help

  • Write a cooking recipe on the text area.
  • When you have finished, click on Analyze to see elements on the dasboard.

    • You may edit your recipe and Analyze as many times as desired.
