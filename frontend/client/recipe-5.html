Personal Dashboard

This dashboard shows some tips to help you improve your recipe.


Tips to improve your recipe:

  • Write the ingredients and the steps in separate sections.
  • List each ingredient separately.
  • List each ingredient separately.
  • Enumerate the steps.
  • Describe how a mixture should look or feel at a certain stage.
  • Give the reasons for doing the actions.
  • Describe until when to do the actions.
  • Specify the form, type and kind of the ingredients when relevant for the recipe
  • Indicate the size and form of cookware if important to recipe results.
  • Indicate whether the cookware is covered or uncovered.
  • Specify the length of cooking time and the kind of heat—low, medium, or high.
  • Adding an ingredient doesn’t indicate mixing or stirring. Do not use “add” if another verb (“stir,” “mix,” “beat,” etc.) is more specific.

    • How to use RELEX

  • First, write a cooking recipe in the Text Editor. Then, click on the "Analyze" button to receive help.
  • In the Personal Dashboard, you will see a list of positive characteristics from recipes taken from "The Recipe Writer's Handbook" (Gibbs & Baker, 2001) to help you improve the writing quality of your recipe.
  • Afterward, edit your recipe accordingly based on the positive characteristics from the list.
