Tips on the Specificity of Steps
Indicate the size of the saucepan or skillet.
Indicate whether the cookware is covered or uncovered.
Specify the intesity of cooking heat.
When appropriate in the recipe method, say, “Prepare according to package directions.”
Use “Salt” and “pepper” as nouns, not verbs.
Indicate the material of the skewers.
Specify the type of pan
When possible, avoid using "cream" as a verb—it can be confused with the ingredient.
Use stir, mix, or beat instead of "add" to be more specific.
Tips for Clarity of Steps
Give the reasons for doing something.
Describe how a mixture should look or feel at a certain stage.
Describe until when to do the actions.

Details

Using Artificial Intelligence, we compare your recipe to other (more than 200,000) recipes written by a range of novice to experienced online users. Based on our model, we select a slightly better recipe from our database. We then show personalized feedback on how to improve the writing quality of your recipe. These suggestions are exemplified in the chosen recipe and were taken from "The Recipe Writer's Handbook" (Gibbs & Baker, 2001).

Help

  • Write a cooking recipe on the Text Editor and click on the "Analyze" button to receive help.
  • In the Personal Dashboard, you will see some tips on how to improve your recipe exemplified in a recipe from another user.
  • These suggestions were taken from "The Recipe Writer's Handbook" (Gibbs & Baker, 2001).
  • Read the example recipe and the tips, then improve your text to include the positive characteristics from the example recipe.
